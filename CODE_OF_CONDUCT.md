# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, technically honest, and willing to distinguish evidence from assumption. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, credential disclosure, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Collaboration

Disagree with ideas and evidence, not people. Ask clarifying questions before assuming intent. Make room for different experience levels. Give credit, explain trade-offs, and correct mistakes openly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content; pause participation; or close issues, pull requests, and discussions that violate this standard. Security or privacy incidents may be handled privately under `SECURITY.md`.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainers through the organization’s GitHub contact path and include links and a concise description. Do not publish sensitive personal information in a public issue.
